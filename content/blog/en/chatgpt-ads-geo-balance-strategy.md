## ChatGPT now has both paid ads and organic visibility—you need a strategy for each

[Search Engine Journal reports on a webinar](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/selling-on-chatgpt-how-brands-are-driving-revenue-through-ads-geo-webinar/589430/) featuring OpenAI, Go Fish Digital, and industry experts on how brands should balance paid and organic visibility in ChatGPT. The core tension is real: some answers show citations only, others show ads, and most marketers are focused on the organic side while paid ads remain a new opportunity. The question for your business is straightforward—which visibility should you buy, and which should you earn? Our recommendation is to audit where your brand appears first, then treat these as two separate channels rather than extensions of traditional search strategy.

## What changed: ChatGPT Ads is not search advertising

OpenAI treats ChatGPT Ads as a new channel, not an add-on to paid search. This distinction matters because the mechanics, audience behavior, and ROI drivers are different. When users ask ChatGPT a question, the response may include citations to websites or paid ads—but these are not the same inventory. A citation in an answer means ChatGPT selected your content as relevant enough to quote or link. An ad means you paid to appear alongside that answer.

Our recommendation is to stop thinking of this as "search with a new interface." ChatGPT users have a different intent pattern than Google search users. They are asking follow-up questions, exploring ideas, and seeking conversation. An ad placed in that context performs differently than a search ad next to a query result.

## The organic side: visibility through GEO requires crawlability first

Organic visibility in ChatGPT starts with how the platform crawls and interprets your pages. If ChatGPT cannot read your content clearly, it cannot cite you. This is where generative engine optimization (GEO) applies.

Our recommendation aligns with what Go Fish Digital's GEO experts are teaching: start by ensuring your site is readable to AI crawlers. [Structure your content so AI answers can actually use it](/blog/structure-content-for-ai-answers). Use clear headings, short paragraphs, and direct language. Avoid ambiguous phrasing. If you want to appear in citations, you need to make it easy for ChatGPT to extract and attribute your insights.

The organic path requires patience and ongoing optimization, but it is sustainable. Citations build trust and authority. Our recommendation is to audit where your brand already appears as a citation in ChatGPT answers. This tells you which topics and page types work and which do not. [Track your brand mentions across LLMs](/blog/track-brand-mentions-across-llms) so you can spot patterns.

## The paid side: when to invest in ChatGPT Ads

Paid ads in ChatGPT are newer, and the targeting and placement options differ from Google Ads or Microsoft Advertising. Abhilash Edathil, Head of SMB Monetization at OpenAI, will share specifics during the webinar, but the basic logic is clear: you pay to appear when ChatGPT generates an answer to a user question.

The timing question is practical. Our recommendation is to invest in ChatGPT Ads when:

- You have identified keywords or topics where you already rank organically but conversion rates suggest paid amplification could help
- You want to test demand for new products or services before building organic authority
- Your competitors are already bidding in ChatGPT Ads and taking share
- You have budget capacity beyond your core search and social channels

Treat this as an incremental revenue channel, not a replacement for anything else. If you are not yet visible in ChatGPT organically, spending on ads before optimizing your content for GEO is inefficient.

## The audit: your starting point

Before allocating budget to either channel, you need to know where you stand. The webinar emphasizes this: audit where your brand appears in ChatGPT today. This means:

- Search for your key products, services, and brand terms in ChatGPT
- Document which of your pages (if any) appear as citations
- Identify topics where competitors are cited but you are not
- Note any paid ads already running for your category
- Check whether ChatGPT mentions your brand in other contexts (competitor comparisons, reviews, etc.)

Our recommendation is to use a structured audit tool that tracks your citations across LLMs, not just ChatGPT. [Find your AI citation gap](/blog/find-your-ai-citation-gap) to understand where you are mentioned everywhere but quoted nowhere. That gap is often where GEO work will give you the highest return.

## How paid and organic work together

The webinar panel will explore this convergence directly. The working hypothesis is that organic visibility (citations) and paid ads (sponsored placements) are complementary, not competing. A user might see your citation in one answer, then see your ad for a related product in another conversation.

Our recommendation is to think about your ChatGPT strategy in phases:

- Phase 1: Audit and optimize for organic visibility (GEO)
- Phase 2: Test paid ads on your best-performing topics
- Phase 3: Scale paid ads while continuously improving organic content quality
- Phase 4: Measure incremental revenue from each channel and adjust

One hypothesis worth testing is whether strong organic presence actually improves paid ad performance, as users may have higher trust in brands they see cited frequently.
