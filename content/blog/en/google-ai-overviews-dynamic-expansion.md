## Google is removing friction from AI Overviews by auto-expanding them for certain queries

[According to Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-is-dynamically-expanding-ai-overviews-for-some-queries-486200), Google has confirmed that AI Overviews will now dynamically expand to their full state for some queries, eliminating the "Show more" button entirely. When expanded, the AI Overview displays as a complete, detailed response and pushes traditional organic search results significantly lower on the page. Google designed the feature to show the full answer without forcing users to click, making the search experience more similar to Google's AI Mode. If a user has already scrolled past the AI Overview, Google cancels the expansion to preserve their reading position.

## What is actually changing on the search results page

Google is removing the need for users to manually reveal the full AI Overview. Instead of showing a truncated snippet with a "Show more" button, the system now displays the complete AI answer in expanded form for select queries. This transformation means organic search results, which previously appeared after a clickable snippet, now require scrolling to reach. The change mimics how AI Mode operates—positioning generated answers as the primary search experience rather than a supplementary feature alongside traditional links.

Google has not stated what percentage of queries trigger this behavior or whether the rollout will expand to additional query types over time.

## Why this matters for your traffic and visibility

The implications are direct: fewer organic clicks. When AI Overviews remain collapsed, users must decide whether to click "Show more" or proceed to organic results. An expanded overview removes that decision point. Users see the full answer without additional effort, reducing the likelihood they click through to any website at all.

Our recommendation is to audit whether your content currently appears in AI Overviews for your target queries. If it does, check the quality of that citation. The expanded format also signals a directional shift: Google is incrementally moving toward AI Mode as the default search experience rather than treating AI-generated answers as optional enhanced features. This aligns with earlier moves, including the introduction of link carousels within AI Overviews for developing topics.

## How to prepare your content strategy

Start by understanding where your content fits in the current AI answer ecosystem. A hypothesis worth testing is that content already structured for AI answers—with clear topic hierarchies, definitions, and structured data—will both appear more frequently and be cited more accurately in these expanded overviews.

The structural decisions you make now determine whether your brand gets included or excluded from these high-visibility answer slots. Consider these steps:

- Audit your top target queries to see if they currently show AI Overviews
- Check whether your domain appears in those overviews and in what capacity (cited, quoted, or merely referenced)
- Review your content structure against how AI systems typically parse information
- Implement schema markup that clarifies the relationships between concepts and data points
- Test whether your content is crawlable by AI indexers and answer engines

## The unresolved questions

Google has not disclosed the rollout timeline, the percentage of queries affected, or whether this expansion represents a permanent shift in how AI Overviews appear globally. A hypothesis is that Google will gradually expand this behavior to additional query categories as it gains confidence in the expanded format's performance.

We cannot yet verify whether certain query types—such as those with high commercial intent or informational depth—are more likely to trigger automatic expansion than others. It is also unclear whether the expanded view will eventually replace the "Show more" interaction entirely or remain query-dependent.

The absence of these specifics makes tracking the business impact difficult. Many publishers rely on Search Console data to measure traffic changes, but distinguishing between losses attributable to AI Overview expansion versus other SERP changes requires closer monitoring.

## What happens next

Google will likely continue this rollout quietly. Search Engine Land noted that Chris Long observed Google revealing the AI Mode prompt box by default for some queries—another signal that Google views AI-generated search as the direction forward.

Publishers should expect that when this feature reaches their key queries, organic clickthrough rates will decline further. The only defense is ensuring your content is structured, marked up, and crawlable in ways that make it *attractive* for inclusion in those expanded answers. That means moving beyond keyword optimization into semantic clarity and information architecture.
