## France got AI Overviews—and we watched the CTR collapse in real time

On July 22, 2026, [France became the latest major search market to get AI Overviews](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-france-impact/). Ahrefs tracked 963 French domains in Google Search Console for 28 days before and 9 days after launch. The result: highly exposed domains lost 23.1% of their click-through rate in nine days. Domains barely exposed lost almost nothing. The relationship held steady across the panel: roughly one percentage point of CTR loss per percentage point of AI Overview exposure. This is not an algorithm update. It is AI Overviews eating traffic directly from your search results.

## Why this matters for your AI visibility

Your ranking position is probably unchanged. Your click-through rate is not. [According to Ahrefs' analysis](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-france-impact/), the most extreme case in their panel had +183% impressions for −1.9% clicks—a tripling of visibility with two-thirds less traffic. This is what we call the great decoupling: an AI Overview does not remove you from the search engine results page. It makes you redundant inside it. The user gets their answer from the overview box. They do not need to click through to your site.

Our recommendation: stop thinking about rankings and start thinking about query intent. [Structure your content so AI answers can actually use it](/blog/structure-content-for-ai-answers), but also understand that short-answer, informational queries are the ones most likely to be absorbed entirely by an AI Overview. A query that ends in a transaction, a tool, an account login, or a human judgment holds up far better.

## The actual distribution was brutal

The median French site lost 5.7% CTR and 8.8% clicks across the full 963-domain panel. But that median hides what [Ahrefs found](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-france-impact/): almost one site in two lost more than 10% of its daily clicks. One in twenty lost half. In nine days. And 82.2% of highly exposed domains lost CTR at all.

French sites were hit harder than international competitors. The .fr domains in the panel lost 12.3% of clicks at the median, against 8.8% overall. French sites are more exposed to AI Overviews in the same search results, meaning they compete in a narrower space.

## Health was the hardest hit

The health sector had the highest AI Overview rate in the study at 22.2%, and its click-through rate fell 20.4%. [This aligns with earlier research](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-france-impact/) finding that 44.1% of medical queries trigger an AI Overview. Health queries are informational. They have short answers. There is no transaction waiting at the end. That is exactly what an AI Overview absorbs best.

But here is what matters for your site: intent beats vertical. A query that a single paragraph can answer is a query you will lose, no matter your industry. A query that needs a tool, a price, a purchase, or professional judgment holds up far better. Before you look at your industry benchmarks, look at your keywords through the lens of user intent.

## How to measure your own exposure—today

Our recommendation: pull your AI Overview exposure rate from your SEO tool before the damage compounds. If you are using Ahrefs, head to the Organic keywords report in Site Explorer and filter by AI Overview in the SERP features section. You will see exactly which of your rankings trigger an AI Overview.

Use that number as a risk factor. If 15% of your queries trigger an AI Overview, expect to lose somewhere around 15% of your CTR—not as a guarantee, but as an order of magnitude. Some heavily exposed sites are still performing fine for reasons we cannot yet verify, and some barely exposed ones are falling apart for other reasons entirely. But across the 963 French domains in this study, the slope held steady at −0.9: one point of exposure cost roughly one point of CTR.

We recommend you:

- Pull your AI Overview exposure rate from your SEO platform today.
- Segment your keywords by user intent: informational vs. transactional vs. navigational.
- Identify which of your high-traffic keywords are informational with short answers.
- Review your top-ranking pages in those categories for how an AI Overview might excerpt them.
- Shift content strategy toward queries that need tools, prices, transactions, or judgment calls.
- Monitor your CTR week-to-week using [our free tools](/free-audit) to catch changes early.

## This will probably get worse

[Ahrefs' global research](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-france-impact/) tracked the same effect over two years. The impact of AI Overviews on position 1 click-through rate went from −34.5% in spring 2025 to −58% by the end of 2025. The impact does not fade with time. It gets worse, as AI Overviews trigger on more queries and users get used to not clicking through.

Nothing in the France data suggests this market will escape that trajectory. The median AI Overview rate among the domains Ahrefs tracked is 5.4%, and 29% are already above 10%. Those numbers have moved in only one direction in every market since 2024. If the global pattern holds, the −23.1% figure we see today will look like the early days of a much larger shift.

We cannot yet verify whether French sites will follow the exact same growth curve as other markets, but the trend is stated as a hypothesis in the data: every market that has received AI Overviews has seen exposure climb, and every market has seen the CTR damage increase over time.

## What you can do right now

The most useful insight from the France data is also the simplest: your risk is measurable, and it is measurable before the damage is done. You do not have to wait nine days to see if AI Overviews will hurt you. You can pull your exposure rate today and know your order of magnitude of risk.

Start by understanding [why AI recommends your competitors instead of you](/blog/why-ai-recommends-your-competitors). Often it is not because your content is worse. It is because your content is not formatted, structured, or cited in a way that an AI model can extract and attribute. Then move to offense: [learn how to measure AI search visibility](/blog/how-to-measure-ai-search-visibility) so you can track what happens to your traffic over the next few months, not years.

The nine days between July 22 and July 31 in France gave us a rare window: a launch date, a before, and an after. What we saw was clear. AI Overviews do not remove you from search. They make you redundant. And that redundancy costs traffic in a way that correlates directly with how many of your queries they answer.

The question is not whether you are exposed. The question is how much, and whether you know it yet.
